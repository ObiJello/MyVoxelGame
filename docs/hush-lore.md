# The Hush — lore bible

The canon for everything written in the Hush: lore books, signs, plaques,
inscriptions, item and block flavour, structure names. Anyone writing a book or
naming a place reads this first and stays inside it. Add to it; do not
contradict it. Where a fact is deliberately left open, it says so — keep it
open (mystery is part of the design).

Engine-side design (blocks, mobs, structures) is in `the-hush.md`; this file
is only the story.

---

## The short version

Long ago a people called **the Choir** learned that sound, held long enough and
pure enough, shapes the world. Stone takes a held note and remembers it;
crystal grows along a chord; light is **song made visible**. They built with
sound.

In the Overworld's deep dark they built outposts — the ancient cities — and
in those cities they built **gates**: the reinforced-deepslate frames. Through
the gates lay **the Hush**, a world so silent that a note sung there never
fades. There the Choir built their capital, **Aurelith, the Lantern City**,
around **the Heart** — a resonance engine as tall as a tower that held one
note, **the Undying Chord**, for more than a thousand years. The Chord lit the
lanterns, grew the crystal, kept the river glowing and held the gates open.

Then the Chord **soured**. What answered it from below the Hush is not known.
The last conductor, **Ysolde**, silenced the Heart — **the Great Rest** — and
sent her people back through the gates. The silence left behind did not stay
empty: **the sculk** grew into it. Sculk is the Hush listening for a song that
stopped. The **Wardens**, the Choir's guardians, were left at their posts;
they still hunt any sound that is not the Chord. Ysolde stayed. The sculk took
her, and she became **the Choir Mother**.

Aurelith still stands. Its lights still burn, because resonant crystal holds
light the way a lung holds breath — slowly letting it go. The city is empty,
and very quietly, it is still singing.

## Names and words

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Choir** | The civilisation. Also each of its guild-families is "a choir" (the Lampwrights' choir, the Tuners' choir). |
| **Aurelith** | Their capital in the Hush. "The Lantern City." Pronounced *AW-reh-lith*. |
| **The Heart** | The great resonance engine at the centre of Aurelith. Formal name: **the Heart of Aurelith**; the engineers called it **the Old Voice**. |
| **The Undying Chord** | The single sustained note the Heart held from the founding to the Great Rest. "The Chord," "the Held Note." |
| **The Great Rest** | The silencing of the Heart and the abandonment of Aurelith. A *rest* is the musical term for a silence. |
| **The Souring** | The years before the Rest when the Chord went wrong. |
| **The Undersong** | What the Choir believed was answering the Chord from beneath the Hush. Never described directly. Keep it unexplained. |
| **The Stave** | The Choir's writing: glyphs set on five horizontal lines. Glowing glyph-stone walls in Aurelith are Stave inscriptions. |
| **Vesper** | The river that runs through Aurelith. Its water glows violet-cyan because it carries the Chord (*vesper-water*, in the engine `resonant_water`). |
| **Echo shards** | "Frozen echoes": a sound so pure it set solid. The Choir's currency and their keys. |
| **Resonant crystal** | Crystal grown by song. It stores light and gives it back. |
| **Echo lanterns** | Resonant crystal caged in metal and "tuned" to glow. The streetlights of every Choir city. |
| **Resonite** | The metal the Choir alloyed from ore that had lain in the song for centuries. Their tools, their cages, their conduits. |
| **Choirstone** | The pale, polished stone Aurelith is built from: hushstone that has been sung smooth. |
| **Lumen panels / lumen strips** | Glowing panels set in walls and streets: song-light in glass. Three colours: cyan (the Chord), violet (the river), amber (the hearths). |
| **The Four Voices** | Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass. The four parts of the Chord; the four gates of Aurelith are named for them. |
| **Wardens** | The Choir's guardians. Bred and grown in the song to hear a *false note* and silence it. Blind because they never needed eyes. |
| **The Silent Warden** | "The warden that never left." Stayed to guard the vaults after the Rest. |
| **The Choir Mother** | Ysolde, the last conductor, transformed by the sculk. She still conducts — the echo wraiths are her choir now. |
| **Echo wraiths** | Voices of the Choir that did not make it through the gates: echoes with nothing left to echo. |
| **Echo mimics** | After-images: a sound that repeats the last person it heard. They fear light because light is song they cannot copy. |
| **Hushlings** | Native Hush creatures, older than the Choir. Harmless. The Choir kept them like songbirds. |
| **Lumen moths** | Native. They drink light. They followed the Choir's lanterns into the city and never left. |
| **Crystal golems** | Hushstone and crystal that the song woke. The Choir used them as builders; now they guard crystal fields out of habit. |
| **The Hush leviathan** | Native. The Choir thought it was the Hush's own voice. It has never made a sound. |
| **The voice keys** | Four tuning keys, one per voice, that seat the Chord in the Conductor's Podium. Taken apart at the Rest and hidden with the guilds so no one could sing the Heart alone. |
| **The Unsung** | The Undersong in the only shape it ever heard: a conductor's. It learned the Chord so well it did not need the Choir to sing it. Keep what it *is* unexplained. |

Conventions:
- The Choir count years **in Chord**: "the 412th year of the Chord" (C. 412).
  The Heart was first sung in C. 1. The Great Rest came in **C. 1147**.
  Nobody in the Hush knows how long ago that was. Never give a figure in
  "our" years.
- A Choir person's name is a given name and their choir: "Orrin of the
  Tuners", "Liesl of the Listeners". Conductors use the given name alone.
- The Choir say "listen" where we say "look": "listen here", "listen to this".
  They say "silence" the way we would say "death" — carefully.
- Mottos cut into stone are in capitals: `WHAT IS SUNG IS NEVER LOST`,
  `LISTEN FIRST`, `HOLD THE NOTE`, `LIGHT IS SONG MADE VISIBLE`,
  `EVERY VOICE A LANTERN`, `REST IS NOT AN ENDING`.

## Aurelith, the Lantern City

A walled city of pale choirstone on a levelled plateau, ~200 blocks across,
cut through by the glowing river Vesper. Wide avenues on a grid, four gate
towers whose beams stand up into the sky (so it can be found from far away),
and at the centre the Heart: enormous crystal rings hanging still in the air
above a dais, turning very slowly, as if something were still keeping time.

It was abandoned mid-life. Tables are still laid in the Stillhouses. Market
stalls still have goods. The Archive's lecterns are still open at the page
someone was reading. Sculk has crept in along the river and up the walls; the
gardens have run wild; one sky bridge has fallen.

### Districts

| District | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **The Plaza of the Held Note** | Centre | The great round plaza around **the Heart**. Four statues of the Four Voices face the dais. The **Conductor's Podium** — an empty raised seat facing the Heart — is where Ysolde stood for the Rest. |
| **The Four Gates** | N, E, S, W edges | **Soprano Gate** (north), **Alto Gate** (east), **Tenor Gate** (south), **Bass Gate** (west). Each a tower with a light beam — one voice of the Chord — standing into the sky. |
| **The Vesper Quays** | Along the river | The harbour: stone quays, mooring posts, a lock basin, arched bridges. Boats of whisperwood still tied up. |
| **The Arcade of Echoes** | Market quarter | A long colonnaded market street. Stalls, counters, the Lampwrights' shop. "Everything here was paid for in echoes." |
| **The Stillhouses** | Residential quarter | Terraced houses of choirstone with dark-glass windows and lumen-strip eaves. Small, calm, domestic. Beds made, tables laid. |
| **The Archive of Echoes** | Library tower | A tall tower of shelves and lecterns: the Listeners kept every voice they could record in echo shards and books. Echo mimics lurk among the shelves (the after-images of the last readers). |
| **Starward** | Observatory | A domed tower where the Choir studied the aurora — which they believed was the Chord's reflection in the sky. |
| **The Quiet Gardens** | Garden terraces | Stepped terraces of whisperwood, lantern leaves and resonance blooms, once kept silent for meditation. Overgrown now. |
| **The Conductor's Spire** | The tallest tower | The seat of the conductors. At the top, an open terrace where you can see the whole city line up: the four gates, the river, the Heart. |
| **The Hall of Instruments** | Near the plaza | The conservatory: note blocks, chimes, resonant crystal "tuning forks". |
| **The Tuners' Works** | Beside the Heart | Where the Heart was maintained. Crystal conduits run from here to the Heart under the plaza. The tuner's logbook lies open. |
| **The Vault of the Chord** | Hidden, under the Heart's dais | The treasury — the Choir's last, best things, sealed at the Rest. |

## How the world works (in lore)

- **Light is song made visible.** Every glowing thing in the Hush — crystal,
  lantern leaves, resonance blooms, echo lanterns — is holding a note.
  Nothing in the Hush burns: there is no fire, no sun. (This is also why the
  Hush has no block light — glow is held, not cast.)
- **The sculk is listening.** Sculk grew into the silence the Heart left. It
  hears everything (sensors) and calls the Wardens (shriekers) because it is
  still trying to find the Chord. Where the sculk is thickest, the Chord was
  loudest.
- **The Wardens hunt a false note.** They were made to find and silence
  discord near the gates and the Heart. Since the Rest every sound is
  discord. They are not evil; they are still on duty.
- **Echo shards open gates** because a gate is a held note; the shard is the
  note, frozen.
- **The aurora is the Chord's afterimage** in the Hush's sky (the Starward
  observers' theory). It is strongest over the Aurora Steppe, where the Choir
  Hall stands.
- **The Stillness** — every few minutes every creature in the Hush freezes
  and the fog closes in. The Choir called this *the Hush drawing breath*.
  They thought it was the world listening for the Chord. It began after the
  Rest.
- **The lighthouses** were raised during the Lantern Age so travellers on the
  plains could find their way; the lamps still turn. They were "tuned to
  notice" travellers: that is why a beam stops and looks at you.

## Aurelith as built

What a player actually finds, so books and signs can point at it
(`tools/gen_aurelith.py`, design in `the-hush.md`):

- **Entering.** Four gates on the city's axes, each two towers and an arch with
  a voice spire over it; the spire's beam stands into the sky in the voice's
  colour (Soprano pale white, Alto cyan, Tenor violet, Bass indigo). The
  keystone over each arch carries the gate's inscription; `we will come back`
  is scratched by the Soprano Gate's inner door, beside the sign for The Last
  Procession.
- **The Heart** stands on its dais at the centre of the round Plaza of the
  Held Note, its rings turning over it; the Four Voices stand on the axes
  facing it (Soprano north, Alto east, Tenor south, Bass west); the Conductor's
  Podium is just north of the dais (Ysolde's plaque, the Charter on a lectern).
  The sculk is thickest in the plaza's inner rings — it grew from here.
- **The way down.** Behind the Bass statue (west), a trapdoor in a ring of
  glowing Stave stone opens on a ladder; a lamp-lit passage runs east under the
  plaza to the **Vault of the Chord** beneath the dais: the spring the Heart was
  raised over (a pool of glowing river water), crystal pillars, three chests,
  Ysolde's "To Whoever Comes After" and her diary on lecterns. "The deep voice
  holds the way down."
- **The Vesper** rises at the west in the Vesper's Spring (a round basin with a
  crystal fountain), runs east under three arched bridges (Canticle Way's and
  the two footbridges) to the harbour — the Vesper Quays, two piers, moored
  boats, Corvin's house and notice — and ends in the Listening Well, a glowing
  shaft of water fifteen deep.
- **The Archive of Echoes** (north-west): a ring tower; a ramp climbs the
  atrium's edge past a gallery every seven blocks; the sealed shards are in a
  chest on the lowest shelf (west side); a sealed reading cell in the wall on
  the third gallery (north) holds "The Undersong". The sky bridge leaves the
  gallery at S + 42 eastward, through a crystal needle (a hatch on the
  needle's top hides a chest), across Canticle Way to the Conductor's Spire.
- **The Conductor's Spire** (north-east of the plaza): the plan of the city is
  laid out in light on its lobby floor; its top terrace (the Conductor's chair,
  Ysolde's diary) is the view point where the four beams, the river and the
  Heart line up.
- **Starward** (east of the Spire): the grand stair up its first step, the
  star-map floor under the dome, Tamsin's observations. The sky bridge from it
  to the needle in the east fell; its middle lies in the street below.
- **The Hall of Instruments** and **the Tuners' Works** are the crescents on
  the plaza's north-west and north-east; the Works' conduit runs under grates
  in the plaza floor to the dais.
- **The Stillhouses** (south-west, on Stillhouse Row): Maren's house is on the
  row's north side, its table still laid, its hearth-lights on, the letter on
  its lectern. **The Arcade of Echoes** (south-east): Hesper's lamp stall is
  stall nine. **The Quiet Gardens** (north-west, against the wall): three
  terraces, a still pool, the Canticle on a lectern.
- **The rooms.** Nothing was cleared when the city left. Flats with beds made
  and a table laid for two, a pot of something by the smoker; the Tuners'
  benches with their crystal stock and the note blocks they tuned against;
  studies of the Listeners' and conductors' quarters, candles on the desks;
  the Arcade's stalls, each still showing its trade (lamps, pottery, crystal,
  candles, cloth, books, glass, food); the gatehouses' bunks; reading desks
  against the Archive's shelves.

### Outside the walls

- **The roads.** A road leaves every gate: the Soprano road is the one the
  Last Procession took — its processional arch still stands over the way a
  short walk out, the lintel fallen across it, `THE LAST PROCESSION / C.1147 /
  passed here` on the city face. The roads are paved at the gates and give
  out into the Hush a few hundred paces on; broken lamp posts line them near
  the city, and here and there a lamp is still lit.
- **The measure.** The Choir measured roads in *measures* — a hundred paces.
  The wall stands one measure from the Heart, so the first stone outside a gate
  is the second. Every road has a mile-marker at each measure, on the right of
  the road going out: a Stave-cut stone, toppled now, the words beneath —
  `SOPRANO ROAD / AURELITH / II MEASURES / KEEP WATCH`, then `LISTEN FIRST`,
  `HOLD THE NOTE`; the Alto road `YOU ARE WELCOME`, `EVERY VOICE`, `A LANTERN`;
  the Tenor road `IT CARRIES`, `FOLLOW THE BEAM`, `LIGHT IS SONG`; the Bass
  road `THE DEEP HOLDS`, `REST, NOT END`, `HUM ONCE`.
- **Along the way.** The outer choirs' farmsteads, roofs fallen in; road watch
  posts; roadside shrines where a traveller stopped to hum (`REST AND
  LISTEN`); a colonnade from a lost roadhouse; cisterns of the river's water.
- **The Garden of Stones** — the Choir's necropolis, off the Bass road ("the
  deep voice holds", and it holds the dead). The dead were carried out by the
  Bass Gate; the Keepers of Rest kept the garden. A precinct wall, statues
  with blank faces along the way, a round chapel where "The Rite of Rest" lies
  open on the lectern, rows of tombs — `HERE RESTS / ALDRIC OF / THE TUNERS`,
  Bryony of the Lampwrights, Cassian of the Quays, Delphine of the Listeners,
  Emeric of the Starward, Fenna of the Stillhouses (`HER NOTE HOLDS`), Gideon
  of the Wardens' Keepers, `HOLLIS, AGED 6 / A SMALL VOICE` — and the
  Conductors' mausoleum: the First Conductor, Severin, Ismay (Ysolde's
  predecessor) and one stone whose name was never cut. **The Rite of Rest**
  (the Keepers of Rest): the dead "come to their rest, as a song does"; carried
  out by the Bass Gate; the family hums the dead one's note once; the small
  crystal at the throat — their voice — is taken back to the Heart, which
  carries it in the Chord ("what is sung is never lost"); the name is cut, the
  face left blank; a lamp is lit at the tomb and burns a hundred years. A last
  page in another hand, C.1147: "We carried no one out tonight. The whole city
  is walking out, and there is no one left to hum for us. Hum for us."
- **The waystation** on the Alto road (most travellers came in by the Alto
  Gate): a walled yard, a hostel with a laid table and bunks, a well, a bell,
  a signpost (`<- AURELITH / two measures / and a half`; `THE STEPPE -> /
  Choir Hall / lighthouses`). **The Waystation Book** (many hands): Pell of the
  Quays walking to the Choir Hall; two Tuners going out to retune the
  lighthouses "from the Tenor. Always the Tenor."; a Stillhouse family whose
  children would not sleep in the city; C.1146, "the lighthouses turn wrong
  now. They stop, and look back toward the city"; and the Keeper's last entry
  on the night of the Procession — "Nobody stopped. Nobody wrote. I am writing
  it for them. The lamp is lit."

### Reawakening the Heart

The Heart can be sung awake. This is canon, and everything written about the
quest stays inside it:

- **Dormant.** Aurelith's lights are low — the crystal has been letting its
  breath out for a thousand years. The Stave bands, panels, strips and lamps
  glow faintly; they are not dead.
- **The four keys.** At the Rest the Heart's voice keys were taken out of the
  Podium and hidden with their guilds, so that no one voice could wake it:
  the **Soprano's** with Liesl, sealed in her reading cell in the Archive's
  wall ("the high one with Liesl"); the **Alto's** in the Hall of
  Instruments' cabinet, tuned shut to the lamplighters' song — *the hearth,
  the river, the Chord, the hearth again* (amber, violet, cyan, amber); the
  **Tenor's** up the tuning mast on the Tuners' Works, where the lighthouses'
  note was set; the **Bass's** in the Vault, "the deep one below", by the
  spring.
- **The order.** A chord is raised as a house is: from the floor to the crown.
  **Bass, Tenor, Alto, Soprano** — deep, bright, warm, high. Every source
  agrees (Orrin's log, Aubade's lesson, the Four Voices, the Podium's
  carving); never write a different order. Sung wrong, the voices clash —
  the discord throws the keys out and the lamps stutter.
- **The awakening.** Sung right, the Chord rises out of the Podium, the rings
  spin up, the four gate beams bend together into one pillar over the Heart,
  and the city's light comes back in a wave from the Heart outward to the
  wall. Then **something below answers**: the Undersong, which had learned
  the Chord while the city slept, rises out of the dais as **the Unsung** and
  steals the Four Voices one by one. Singing a voice back at its statue, and
  the Heart's own pulse, break its hold.
- **After.** The Chord is held again. The Heart gives the singer **the Held
  Note** — a little of the Chord to carry ("a little of it is yours now");
  sounded, it holds the Hush's hostile things still, as the Chord once held
  the city. Ysolde's **Coda** lies open on the Podium: the Rest was the one
  note the Undersong could not copy — a silence — and she stayed because
  someone had to keep it. The city stays lit, and nothing hostile is born
  inside its walls again. `WE CAME BACK.`
