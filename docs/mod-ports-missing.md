# Twilight Forest and Aether — what is not ported yet

Generated 2026-09-22 by diffing each mod's own registries (generated blockstates, item
models, `TFEntities.java`, `AetherEntityTypes.java`) against the engine's block, item and
entity tables. Spawn eggs and armour-trim model variants are left out. Regenerate the
lists with the diff script before trusting them after later passes.

## Systems and mechanics (both mods)

- **Bosses.** TF: naga, lich (+ minions, bolts, bombs), minoshroom, hydra (+ mortars),
  knight phantoms, ur-ghast, alpha yeti, snow queen (+ ice cores), plateau boss.
  Aether: slider, valkyrie queen, sun spirit. Arenas generate; the boss spot only logs
  `PENDING BOSS`. Boss spawner blocks, trophies, banner patterns and music discs follow.
- **Player riding.** Saddled phygs, flying cows, moas, and the TF/Aether boats cannot be
  ridden — the engine has no player riding. Swet swallowing, yeti grabs and pinch-beetle
  clamps on players wait on the same thing.
- **Status effects on players.** Players carry no effects, so poison needles, echo blades,
  Aether remedy/inebriation and TF potion flasks only act on mobs.
- **Accessories (Aether).** Gloves, rings, pendants, capes, golden feather, iron bubble,
  regeneration stone, shield of repulsion, invisibility cloak: needs an accessory slot UI.
- **Aether utility blocks.** Altar (enchanting/repair), freezer, incubator (moa eggs), sun
  altar (time control), treasure chest (locked, key-opened), chest mimic.
- **TF progression.** Magic/maze/ore maps and their foci, the uncrafting table menu, the
  advancement-locked biomes (fog/snow/heat/darkness gates), quest ram, charms of life and
  keeping, the tower key and vanishing/reappearing/locked blocks, the ghast trap, the
  carminite reactor, force fields, the trophy pedestal.
- **Remaining TF landmarks.** Dark tower, knight stronghold, labyrinth, hydra lair, yeti
  cave, aurora palace, troll cave, final castle, giant house, mushroom tower, camp,
  fallen trunk (they log "not yet ported").
- **Mob spawner blocks**
  (inert), **thorns contact damage**, **worn armour on players/mobs** (armour stands only),
  **sounds and music** (no sound system), **block light** (glowing blocks don't light
  their surroundings), TF's per-biome sky light colour and coloured leaf tints.
